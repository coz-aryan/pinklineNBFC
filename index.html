FAQs

Common questions

How much can I borrow against my property?

Usually up to 60% of the market value of a residential property and up to 50% for commercial property, subject to your income and repayment capacity.

Can I keep living in or using the mortgaged property?

Yes. You keep full use and ownership. We hold the original property documents until the loan is closed, then return them.

Do I need an income tax return to apply?

Not always. For small business owners we can assess income from bank statements, business records and a personal discussion.

Are there charges for closing my loan early?

Individual borrowers on floating-rate loans taken for non-business purposes pay no foreclosure charges, as per RBI guidelines. Other cases are set out in your sanction letter.

About Pinkline Finance

Credit that respects what you've built

Pinkline Finance Company Private Limited is a Jaipur-based non-banking finance company focused on secured loans. We lend against residential and commercial property to self-employed people, small businesses and salaried families in Rajasthan.

Our story

Many hardworking borrowers in Rajasthan own valuable property but struggle to get fair credit, because their income is in cash or their paperwork does not fit a bank's checklist. We started Pinkline Finance to close that gap.

We take the time to understand each borrower's business and property, and we price loans on real risk rather than on a rigid template. Every loan is secured, documented and explained in plain language.

Mission

Give property owners in Rajasthan fair, transparent access to credit that helps them grow.

Vision

Be the most trusted secured lender for small businesses and families in North India.

Regulated & responsible

Registered with the Reserve Bank of India as an NBFC. We follow RBI's Fair Practices Code, KYC norms and digital lending guidelines.
